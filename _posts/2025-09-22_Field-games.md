In summer 2025, I was the Field Games Director at YMCA Camp Sloper. It was my job to come up with fun outdoor games for kids ages 5-15 to play.

### The Big Book of Games

This is a resource I created at the beginning of the summer, taking an amalgomation of games from various other websites along with ones I created or rewrote the directions for myself. *While admittedly not the best practice,* there may be some directions that are copy-paste from other websites to save myself a lot of time before the summer started.

Some awesome sources I used to compile The Big Book of Games include:

-   https://ultimatecampresource.com/
-   https://www.ssww.com/blog/
-   https://healthbeet.org/educators/pe-teacher/

Although I did not use many of these games this summer, I am sure many other people will find this list to be helpful for planning out their own outdoor summer games.
