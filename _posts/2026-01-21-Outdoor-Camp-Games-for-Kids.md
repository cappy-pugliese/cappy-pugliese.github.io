In summer 2025, I was the Field Games Director at YMCA Camp Sloper. It was my job to come up with fun outdoor games for kids ages 5-15 to play.
<br>
<br>
### The Big Book of Games

This is a resource I created at the beginning of the summer, taking an amalgomation of games from various other websites along with ones I created or rewrote the directions for myself. *While admittedly not the best practice,* some directions remain unchanged from their original source (to save myself a lot of time before the summer started). Therefore, I do not take credit for writing the directions for all of these games.
